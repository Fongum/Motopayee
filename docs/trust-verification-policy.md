# MotoPayee Trust And Verification Policy

Reference date: August 2026

## Core Trust Principle

MotoPayee should not promise that every vehicle is verified or inspected.

MotoPayee should make each trust level visible, honest, and easy for buyers, sellers, dealers, rental owners, renters, and finance partners to understand.

## Trust Promise

MotoPayee helps reduce risk by reviewing listings, verifying information where possible, supporting inspections, marking finance-eligible vehicles clearly, and managing structured rental booking flows.

MotoPayee does not guarantee every transaction, every vehicle condition, every seller claim, or every financing outcome.

## Trust Labels

MotoPayee may use these labels:

- MotoPayee Reviewed
- Seller Verified
- Documents Checked
- Inspected by MotoPayee
- Finance Eligible
- Verified Rental
- Trusted Dealer

Each label must mean something specific.

## MotoPayee Reviewed

Meaning:

MotoPayee has checked that the listing has enough basic information to appear publicly.

Minimum requirements:

- Seller or dealer contact captured internally
- Vehicle make, model, year, city, and price provided
- Photos are usable
- Seller is reachable
- Basic spam or obvious fraud check passed
- Listing does not make false claims about verification, inspection, or financing

What it does not mean:

- It does not mean documents are verified.
- It does not mean the seller is fully verified.
- It does not mean the vehicle has been inspected.
- It does not mean the vehicle is finance eligible.

Suggested public wording:

This listing has been reviewed by MotoPayee before publication. Documents and inspection may still be pending.

## Seller Verified

Meaning:

MotoPayee has checked the seller identity or seller profile information.

Minimum requirements:

- Seller name captured
- Phone/WhatsApp confirmed
- ID or business identity checked where available
- Seller role understood: individual, dealer, owner, agent, or representative

What it does not mean:

- It does not automatically prove vehicle ownership.
- It does not mean the vehicle condition has been checked.
- It does not mean financing is available.

Suggested public wording:

MotoPayee has checked the seller identity or profile information.

## Documents Checked

Meaning:

MotoPayee has reviewed available ownership or vehicle documents.

Minimum requirements:

- Ownership or authority-to-sell document received
- Vehicle identity reasonably matches the listing
- Seller authority is plausible
- Any major document concern is recorded internally

What it does not mean:

- It does not replace legal transfer procedures.
- It does not guarantee that no hidden claims exist.
- It does not mean the vehicle condition has been inspected.

Suggested public wording:

MotoPayee has reviewed available vehicle or ownership documents.

## Inspected By MotoPayee

Meaning:

MotoPayee staff has reviewed the vehicle condition and created a condition summary.

Minimum requirements:

- MotoPayee staff inspection completed
- Exterior condition reviewed
- Interior condition reviewed
- Mileage/odometer observed where available
- Visible issues noted
- Condition summary written
- Inspection date recorded

What it does not mean:

- It does not guarantee the vehicle has no hidden mechanical issue.
- It does not replace a full mechanical workshop diagnosis unless explicitly stated.
- It does not guarantee future performance.

Suggested public wording:

MotoPayee has inspected this vehicle and prepared a condition summary.

## Finance Eligible

Meaning:

MotoPayee has enough confidence to route the vehicle to financing partners for buyer applications.

Minimum requirements:

- Listing reviewed
- Seller verified or verification in progress with acceptable confidence
- Documents checked where available
- Vehicle inspected or condition sufficiently summarized
- Asking price reviewed for reasonableness
- Vehicle type, condition, and price likely fit partner rules

What it does not mean:

- It does not guarantee loan approval.
- It does not mean MotoPayee is the lender.
- It does not mean all finance partners will accept the application.

Suggested public wording:

This vehicle may be used for a financing application through MotoPayee partner institutions. Final approval is subject to partner review.

## Verified Rental

Meaning:

MotoPayee has reviewed the rental owner, vehicle information, rates, deposit, and rental terms before publishing.

Minimum requirements:

- Owner identity checked
- Authority to rent checked
- Vehicle details complete
- Photos received
- Rate confirmed
- Security deposit confirmed
- Rental mode confirmed: self-drive, chauffeur, or both
- Insurance status recorded
- Availability status recorded
- Rental terms accepted by owner

What it does not mean:

- It does not guarantee renter behavior.
- It does not guarantee no dispute will occur.
- It does not mean MotoPayee holds the security deposit unless separately stated.

Suggested public wording:

MotoPayee has reviewed this rental vehicle, owner details, rate, deposit, and basic rental terms.

## Trusted Dealer

Meaning:

MotoPayee has approved the dealer for the pilot or partner program and the dealer has agreed to marketplace standards.

Minimum requirements:

- Dealer/business name captured
- Owner or manager contact confirmed
- Inventory contact person assigned
- Dealer agrees to listing accuracy
- Dealer agrees to update sold or unavailable vehicles
- Dealer agrees to lead handling expectations
- Dealer agrees not to make false financeable claims

What it does not mean:

- It does not mean every dealer vehicle is inspected.
- It does not mean every dealer vehicle is finance eligible.
- It does not mean MotoPayee guarantees every dealer transaction.

Suggested public wording:

This dealer is part of the MotoPayee dealer program and has agreed to marketplace standards.

## Unverified Listings

Unverified listings may appear publicly only after MotoPayee review.

Unverified listings:

- Can receive buyer interest through MotoPayee
- Can be upgraded to verified or inspected
- Should not show direct seller contact by default
- Should not claim financing eligibility
- Should not be ranked above verified or inspected listings

Suggested public wording:

This vehicle has not yet completed MotoPayee verification. We recommend requesting document review or inspection before payment.

## Buyer-Requested Inspection

Buyers may request inspection for vehicles they are interested in.

Inspection request rules:

- Inspection fee should be stated upfront
- Payment status should be recorded
- Inspection date and location should be recorded
- Inspector or staff owner should be assigned
- Condition summary should be recorded
- Buyer should understand that inspection reduces risk but does not eliminate all risk

Recommended launch fee:

- Starting around 15,000 XAF, adjustable by city, distance, and inspection depth

## Staff Approval Checklist

Before applying MotoPayee Reviewed:

- Listing details complete
- Photos usable
- Seller reachable
- Price present
- City/location present
- No obvious fake or spam signal

Before applying Seller Verified:

- Seller identity checked
- Phone confirmed
- Seller role understood

Before applying Documents Checked:

- Ownership or authority document reviewed
- Vehicle identity reasonably matches listing
- Document concern logged if any

Before applying Inspected by MotoPayee:

- Staff inspection completed
- Condition summary written
- Visible issues recorded
- Inspection date recorded

Before applying Finance Eligible:

- Listing reviewed
- Seller trust acceptable
- Documents checked where available
- Condition inspected or summarized
- Price reviewed
- Vehicle likely fits partner rules

Before applying Verified Rental:

- Owner identity checked
- Authority to rent checked
- Rate and deposit confirmed
- Photos received
- Insurance status recorded
- Terms confirmed

Before applying Trusted Dealer:

- Dealer identity captured
- Dealer contact confirmed
- Pilot or partner terms accepted
- Lead handling expectations accepted

## Claims MotoPayee Must Avoid

Do not say:

- Every car is verified.
- Every car is inspected.
- Financing is guaranteed.
- MotoPayee guarantees the transaction.
- No risk.
- Cheapest cars in Cameroon.
- All sellers are trusted.
- All rental deposits are protected by MotoPayee.
- MotoPayee is the lender.

Use instead:

- Reviewed listings
- Verified where marked
- Inspected vehicles are clearly labeled
- Financing subject to partner review and approval
- MotoPayee helps reduce risk
- Rental payment and terms are structured through MotoPayee

## Escalation Rules

Escalate to the founder before publishing or approving if:

- Ownership is unclear
- Seller seems suspicious
- Vehicle price looks unusually low or misleading
- Dealer refuses lead handling expectations
- Rental owner refuses deposit clarity
- Finance eligibility is uncertain
- Buyer or renter reports pressure to pay outside MotoPayee
- Documents appear altered or inconsistent

## Public Trust Summary

MotoPayee helps buyers, sellers, dealers, rental owners, renters, and finance partners transact with more confidence by making trust levels clear.

The trust system is only valuable if labels are used honestly.
