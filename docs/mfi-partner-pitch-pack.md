# MotoPayee Finance Partner Program

Reference date: August 2026

## Positioning

MotoPayee is a professional trusted vehicle marketplace for Cameroon.

Operational finance-partner rollout starts in Buea and Douala, then expands city by city as partner coverage becomes reliable.

MotoPayee does not lend money. MotoPayee prepares and routes organized vehicle finance applications attached to reviewed and finance-eligible vehicles.

## Partner Opportunity

Vehicle finance demand exists, but many applications are difficult for finance institutions to process because buyer information, vehicle details, seller credibility, collateral information, and supporting documents are often incomplete or scattered.

MotoPayee helps finance partners receive cleaner applications connected to better-reviewed vehicle opportunities.

## The Problem For Finance Institutions

Vehicle finance applications often come with:

- Incomplete buyer information
- Weak source-of-income documentation
- Unclear seller identity
- Unverified vehicle ownership
- Unclear vehicle condition
- Poor collateral visibility
- Slow document follow-up
- High time spent on low-quality leads

This makes vehicle finance slower, riskier, and more expensive to process.

## MotoPayee Solution

MotoPayee creates a structured marketplace layer before the finance institution reviews the file.

MotoPayee organizes:

- Reviewed vehicle listings
- Seller and document verification where available
- Inspection badge and condition summary where completed
- Finance-eligible vehicle status
- Buyer profile and contact information
- Source-of-income documents
- Collateral and human surety information
- Buyer consent to share application with finance partners

The finance partner keeps its own credit decision. MotoPayee improves the quality and structure of the application package.

## What Finance-Eligible Means

A vehicle should be marked finance eligible only when MotoPayee has enough confidence to route it to partner institutions.

Minimum checks:

- Listing reviewed by MotoPayee
- Seller identity checked or in progress
- Ownership documents checked where available
- Vehicle condition inspected or sufficiently summarized
- Asking price reviewed for reasonableness
- Vehicle type, year, condition, and price likely fit partner rules

Important boundary:

Finance eligible does not mean financing is guaranteed. Final approval remains subject to partner review.

## Buyer Application Data Collected

MotoPayee can collect:

- Buyer full name
- Phone and WhatsApp
- City
- Preferred vehicle
- Down payment available
- Buying timeline
- Source of income
- Income documents
- Land or house collateral details where applicable
- Human surety details
- National ID or passport where required
- Consent to share application with selected finance partners

## Partner Response Workflow

1. Buyer chooses a finance-eligible vehicle.
2. Buyer submits one structured MotoPayee finance application.
3. MotoPayee checks completeness.
4. Application is routed to selected finance partners.
5. Partners review and respond.
6. Buyer sees partner names and available responses.
7. Buyer selects preferred partner.
8. Selected partner continues normal credit process.
9. MotoPayee tracks status and outcome.

## Partner Response Options

Partners may respond with:

- Interested
- Need more documents
- Conditional approval
- Declined
- Offer terms
- Final approval
- Disbursed

## Why Partners Benefit

Finance partners receive:

- Access to motivated vehicle buyers
- Better-organized application files
- Vehicle information attached to the application
- Seller and trust status context
- Lower customer acquisition cost
- Faster document review
- Competitive visibility inside MotoPayee
- A digital trail for application status

## Pilot Proposal

MotoPayee recommends a 3-6 month pilot with selected microfinance institutions, credit unions, and dealer-finance partners.

Pilot scope:

- Buea and Douala first
- No monthly partner fee during pilot
- MotoPayee sends only finance-eligible vehicle applications
- Multiple partners may compete/respond
- Partner commits to a response timeline
- Success commission applies only after disbursement

Recommended response timeline:

- Initial response within 48-72 hours for complete applications
- Clear request for missing documents when needed
- Final decision timeline defined by partner policy

## Commercial Model

Early model:

- No monthly fee during pilot
- No charge for incomplete exploratory conversations
- Success commission paid only when financing is disbursed

Future options after pilot:

- Qualified lead fee
- Featured partner placement
- Partner portal subscription
- Dealer-finance processing partnership

MotoPayee recommends starting with success commission because it aligns incentives and reduces risk for the partner.

## What MotoPayee Needs From Partner

To route better applications, MotoPayee needs:

- Vehicle finance eligibility rules
- Minimum and maximum loan amounts
- Required down payment
- Tenor range
- Interest and fee guidance
- Required borrower documents
- Accepted source-of-income documents
- Collateral requirements
- Human surety requirements
- Branch/contact person for applications
- Response timeline
- Commercial terms for successful disbursement

## Meeting Questions For Partner

1. What vehicle types do you currently finance?
2. What buyer profiles are strongest for approval?
3. What is your minimum down payment?
4. What loan amount range is acceptable?
5. What repayment tenors do you allow?
6. What source-of-income documents do you require?
7. Do you accept land or house assets as collateral support?
8. How do you evaluate human sureties?
9. How fast can you respond to a complete application?
10. Are you comfortable with competitive partner responses?
11. Are you open to a success commission after disbursement?

## Suggested Meeting Script

Opening:

MotoPayee is building a trusted vehicle marketplace for Cameroon. We help buyers find reviewed and finance-eligible vehicles, then submit structured applications to selected finance partners.

Important clarification:

MotoPayee is not asking you to finance random online listings. Financing is only enabled on vehicles that pass our finance-eligible process.

Partner value:

Our goal is to reduce the work your team spends chasing incomplete applications. We collect buyer information, income documents, collateral and surety details, and attach vehicle information before sending the file to you.

Boundary:

You keep your credit decision. MotoPayee prepares and routes better applications.

Pilot ask:

We would like to start with a 3-6 month pilot where your institution receives structured applications and pays a success commission only when a loan is disbursed.

## Compliance And Trust Boundaries

MotoPayee should always state:

- Financing is subject to partner review and approval.
- MotoPayee is not the lender.
- Buyer data is shared only with consent.
- Partner institutions keep their own underwriting decision.
- Finance-eligible vehicle status does not guarantee loan approval.
