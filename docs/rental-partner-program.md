# MotoPayee Rental Partner Program

Reference date: August 2026

## Positioning

MotoPayee is building a trusted vehicle rental network for Cameroon.

MotoPayee helps vehicle owners and rental operators earn from verified rental vehicles while MotoPayee supports visibility, booking payment, and structured rental terms.

## Who Can Join

MotoPayee can onboard:

- Private vehicle owners
- Rental companies
- Chauffeur vehicle operators
- SUVs for travel and family use
- Vans and buses for groups
- Trucks for business and logistics
- Wedding and event vehicles
- Corporate rental providers

## Why Rental Partners Should Join

Rental partners need visibility, trusted renter intake, clear booking records, and structured payment handling.

MotoPayee helps rental partners:

- Reach renters across Cameroon
- Prepare professional rental listings
- Show verified rental status
- Handle booking payment
- Record security deposit terms
- Capture renter details
- Clarify self-drive or chauffeur terms
- Promote vehicles during peak travel and event seasons

## What MotoPayee Provides

During launch, MotoPayee can provide:

- Rental listing review
- Vehicle profile creation
- Rental inquiry routing
- Booking payment handling
- Deposit record keeping
- Renter intake support
- Basic rental terms structure
- WhatsApp and social promotion for selected vehicles

## What Rental Owners Must Provide

Rental owners should provide:

- Owner or business name
- Phone and WhatsApp
- City and vehicle location
- Vehicle make, model, and year
- Vehicle type
- Plate number
- Photos
- Rental mode: self-drive, with driver, or both
- Daily, weekly, or monthly rate
- Security deposit amount
- Availability dates
- Insurance status
- Ownership or authority-to-rent proof
- Rental restrictions and conditions

## Verification Before Publishing

Rental vehicles should not go live until MotoPayee verifies the minimum information.

Minimum checks:

- Owner identity checked
- Authority to rent checked
- Vehicle details complete
- Photos received
- Rate and deposit confirmed
- Insurance status recorded
- Self-drive or chauffeur terms clear
- Location confirmed
- MotoPayee approval to publish

## Payment Rules

MotoPayee handles booking payment for rental transactions.

Recommended launch model:

- Renter pays rental amount through MotoPayee.
- MotoPayee records the booking.
- MotoPayee deducts commission from the rental amount.
- Owner payout happens after booking confirmation or pickup confirmation, depending on the agreed process.
- Security deposit terms are recorded before the rental begins.

## Security Deposit Rules

The vehicle owner holds the security deposit.

MotoPayee should still record:

- Deposit amount
- Deposit payment date
- Who received the deposit
- Refund conditions
- Damage or late return conditions
- Renter acknowledgement
- Owner confirmation

Deposit disputes are more likely when terms are unclear. The deposit amount and refund rules must be stated before the renter confirms the booking.

## Self-Drive Requirements

Self-drive rentals carry higher risk than chauffeur rentals.

Minimum renter requirements:

- Valid driver's license
- National ID or passport
- Phone and WhatsApp
- Emergency contact
- Rental dates
- Pickup and dropoff location
- Acceptance of rental and deposit terms

Recommended additional controls:

- Mileage limit
- Extra kilometer charge
- Allowed routes or cities
- Late return fee
- Fuel return condition
- Vehicle handover checklist

## Chauffeur Rental Requirements

For chauffeur rentals, the renter may not need a driver's license, but MotoPayee should still capture:

- Renter name
- Phone and WhatsApp
- ID or passport where needed
- Trip purpose
- Rental dates and hours
- Pickup and dropoff location
- Overtime terms
- Cancellation terms

The vehicle owner or operator should confirm:

- Driver availability
- Driver contact
- Vehicle availability
- Rate
- Overtime fee
- Fuel terms

## Damage, Late Return, And Cancellation Principles

MotoPayee should keep rental rules clear before payment.

Damage:

- Vehicle condition should be noted before pickup.
- Damage responsibility should be clear.
- Deposit deduction conditions should be stated.

Late return:

- Late return fees should be defined upfront.
- Extra day rules should be clear.

Cancellation:

- Cancellation window should be stated.
- Refund rules should be stated.
- No-show handling should be stated.

## Commission Model

Recommended launch model:

- MotoPayee commission: 10% of rental booking value
- Optional renter service fee later
- Featured rental placement later
- Owner verification fee later if needed

Start with commission because it aligns MotoPayee revenue with successful bookings.

Each rental listing should show:

- Vehicle type
- Make, model, and year
- City/location
- Daily rate

- Minimum rental days

- Availability status
