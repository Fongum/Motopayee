# MotoPayee Launch Board Manual

Reference date: August 2026

## Executive Summary

MotoPayee is positioned as a trusted vehicle marketplace for Cameroon.

The first launch cycle should focus on:

- Trusted vehicle buying and selling
- Vehicle financing through partner institutions
- Verified rentals
- Free supply acquisition from individual sellers and dealers
- Professional marketplace trust standards

Imports remain secondary during this strategy cycle.

## Strategic Decisions

### Public Positioning

MotoPayee should say:

MotoPayee is the trusted vehicle marketplace for Cameroon.

MotoPayee should not publicly limit itself to Buea and Douala, because sellers, dealers, rental owners, and buyers from other cities may want to join.

### Internal Operating Focus

Operational priority starts in key launch cities and expands city by city.

This means:

- Accept inquiries from across Cameroon
- Prioritize verification and inspection where the team can operate reliably
- Do not reject useful leads from Yaounde, Limbe, Bamenda, or other cities
- Be honest when verification timing depends on city coverage

| Area | Target |
| --- | ---: |
| Sale listings | 25+ |

| Dealer pilots | 3-5 |

| WhatsApp Business channel | 1 dedicated channel |
| Inspection package | 1 clear offer |

## Team Operating Model

Team:

- Founder
- Staff 1
- Staff 2

Role split:

| Person | Primary Responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| Founder | Dealers, finance partners, key partnerships |
| Staff 1 | Seller and rental owner onboarding |
| Staff 2 | Listing packaging, data entry, verification follow-up |

Weekly rhythm:

- Monday: scorecard review and assignments
- Tuesday-Wednesday: outreach and intake
- Thursday: listing packaging and verification review
- Friday: lead follow-up and launch content preparation
- Saturday: inspections, viewings, rental checks
- Sunday: light reporting only

## Launch Readiness Gate

Start buyer/renter public promotion only when:

- 25+ sale listings are presentable
- 20+ rental vehicles are presentable or clearly coming online
- Inquiry handling is ready
- Trust labels are clear
- WhatsApp process is working
- Rental payment and deposit rules are defined
- Inspection package is ready

If targets are not ready, continue with supply-side campaigns:

- List your car free
- Register your rental vehicle
- Join the dealer pilot

## Trust System

MotoPayee should make trust levels visible and honest.

Trust labels:

- MotoPayee Reviewed
- Seller Verified
- Documents Checked
- Inspected by MotoPayee
- Finance Eligible
- Verified Rental
- Trusted Dealer

Core rule:

MotoPayee should not promise that every vehicle is verified or inspected. It should clearly show which trust level applies.

## Commercial Model

Launch free where MotoPayee needs supply and partner participation.

Charge where MotoPayee creates measurable value:

- Inspection
- Featured visibility
- Rental booking payment
- Financing success
- Premium transaction support

Recommended launch defaults:

| Item | Launch Policy |
| --- | --- |
| Basic sale listing | Free |
| Dealer pilot | Free for 6 months |
| Document verification | Free at first |
| Buyer-requested inspection | 15,000 XAF starting point |
| Seller inspection package | 15,000-35,000 XAF |
| Featured listing | 10,000 XAF starting point |
| Rental commission | 10% |
| Financing application | Free to buyer |
| Financing revenue | Success commission from partner |
| Insurance quote | Free to user |
| Import fees | Future/secondary |

## Dealer Strategy

Dealers are the fastest path to inventory volume.

Dealer pilot:

- Free for 6 months
- Selected dealers across Cameroon
- MotoPayee helps package inventory
- Dealer provides accurate prices, photos, and updates
- MotoPayee controls trust labels and finance eligibility
- MotoPayee-generated leads should be handled through MotoPayee during pilot

## Financing Strategy

MotoPayee should not position itself as a lender.

Positioning:

MotoPayee prepares and routes structured vehicle finance applications attached to reviewed and finance-eligible vehicles.

Partner targets:

- Microfinance institutions
- Credit unions
- Dealer-finance partners

Commercial model:

- Free partner pilot
- Success commission after disbursement
- Partner keeps underwriting decision

Important claim:

Financing is subject to partner review and approval.

## Rental Strategy

Rental is a strong early revenue lane because MotoPayee handles booking payment.

Rental categories:

- Private cars
- SUVs
- Vans
- Buses
- Trucks
- Chauffeur vehicles
- Wedding and event vehicles
- Corporate rentals

Rental model:

- Rental vehicle must be verified before public listing
- Renter pays booking amount through MotoPayee
- MotoPayee deducts commission
- Owner holds security deposit
- MotoPayee records deposit terms and confirmation

Launch commission:

- 10% of rental booking value

## Public Messaging

Primary line:

MotoPayee: The trusted vehicle marketplace for Cameroon.

Supporting lines:

- Buy reviewed vehicles.
- Sell your car for free.
- Access finance-eligible cars.
- Book verified rentals.
- Join the dealer pilot.
- Register your rental vehicle.

Do not say:

- Guaranteed financing
- All cars are inspected
- All listings are verified
- No risk
- Cheapest cars in Cameroon
- MotoPayee is the lender

## 90-Day Review

After 90 days, review:

- Listings published
- Verified listings
- Inspections requested
- Dealer pilots active
- Rental vehicles approved
- Rental bookings
- Financing applications
- Finance partner responses
- Revenue from inspections, rentals, and promotions
- Buyer trust feedback
- Seller and dealer willingness to pay
- Bypass patterns

The next strategy cycle should be based on real activity, not assumptions.

## Board Conclusion

MotoPayee should launch as a professional, trusted vehicle marketplace for Cameroon.

The strategy is:

- Grow supply with free listings and free dealer pilots
- Protect the brand with clear trust labels
- Monetize trust, rentals, financing success, and premium visibility
- Keep imports secondary until the local marketplace, financing, and rentals are stronger
- Track execution weekly and adjust after evidence
