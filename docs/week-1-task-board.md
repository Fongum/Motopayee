# MotoPayee Week 1 Task Board

Reference date: August 2026

## Week 1 Objective

Set up the launch operating system and start supply-side outreach.

By the end of week 1, MotoPayee should have:

- WhatsApp Business ready
- First seller outreach started
- First rental owner outreach started
- First dealer conversations started
- First finance partner conversations started
- At least 5 draft sale listings
- At least 5 rental leads

## Founder Tasks

### WhatsApp Business Setup

- Set up dedicated MotoPayee WhatsApp Business number.
- Add MotoPayee logo.
- Add business profile description.
- Add contact details.
- Add quick replies.
- Add lead labels.

Recommended profile text:

MotoPayee helps people in Cameroon buy, sell, finance, and rent vehicles with more confidence. List your vehicle for free, register a rental vehicle, join the dealer pilot, or ask about finance-eligible cars.

Recommended labels:

- Seller lead
- Dealer lead
- Rental owner
- Buyer lead
- Renter lead
- Finance partner
- Inspection request
- Waiting for photos
- Waiting for documents
- Follow-up
- Published
- Closed

### Dealer Outreach

- Build initial dealer list.
- Contact first 5 dealers.
- Schedule at least 2 dealer conversations.
- Ask each dealer about available inventory.
- Introduce free 6-month dealer pilot.

Dealer target:

- 5 dealers contacted
- 2 follow-up meetings or calls scheduled

### Finance Partner Outreach

- Build initial MFI/credit union/dealer-finance list.
- Contact first 3 finance partners.
- Send or present MotoPayee finance partner positioning.
- Request meeting to discuss pilot.

Finance partner target:

- 3 finance partners contacted
- 1 meeting scheduled if possible

## Staff 1 Tasks

### Seller Outreach

- Contact private sellers through WhatsApp, referrals, Facebook, and field contacts.
- Explain free listing.
- Collect basic vehicle details.
- Request photos and price.
- Record every lead in tracker.

Seller target:

- 10 seller contacts
- 5 seller intakes started

### Rental Owner Outreach

- Contact private owners, rental operators, chauffeur owners, event vehicle owners, vans, buses, and trucks.
- Explain verified rental onboarding.
- Collect vehicle type, rate, deposit, availability, and photos.
- Record every lead in tracker.

Rental target:

- 10 rental owner contacts
- 5 rental leads started

## Staff 2 Tasks

### Intake Sheets And Tracking

- Set up lead tracker using the Launch Dashboard Specification.
- Create tabs or sections for:
  - Weekly Scorecard
  - Lead Tracker
  - Sale Listings Pipeline
  - Rental Pipeline
  - Dealer Pipeline
  - MFI Pipeline
  - Inspection Tracker
  - Launch Readiness

### Listing Packaging

- Package existing seller inventory.
- Prepare at least 5 draft sale listings.
- Check each draft for:
  - make
  - model
  - year
  - city
  - price
  - mileage if available
  - photos
  - seller contact
  - trust status

Listing target:

- 5 draft sale listings ready for review

### Document Organization

- Create folders or tracker links for:
  - seller photos
  - rental photos
  - dealer inventory
  - verification documents
  - MFI contacts

## Daily Rhythm

Morning:

- Review yesterday's leads.
- Assign follow-ups.
- Confirm outreach targets for the day.

Midday:

- Outreach calls/messages.
- Collect details and photos.
- Update lead statuses.

Evening:

- Review new leads.
- Move each lead to a status.
- Identify missing photos, prices, or documents.
- Prepare next-day follow-ups.

## Daily Lead Status Rule

Every lead must end each day in one status:

- New
- Contacted
- Waiting for photos
- Waiting for price
- Waiting for documents
- Ready for review
- Approved
- Published
- Rejected
- Follow-up later
- Closed

## Blockers To Escalate

Escalate to founder immediately if:

- Seller cannot prove authority to sell
- Dealer refuses lead handling expectations
- Rental owner refuses deposit clarity
- Vehicle price looks suspicious
- Buyer or seller wants to bypass MotoPayee immediately
- Documents appear altered or inconsistent
- Staff cannot reach a lead after repeated attempts
- A finance partner asks for unclear commercial terms

## Friday Review Checklist

Review:

- How many seller contacts were made?
- How many rental owner contacts were made?
- How many dealers were contacted?
- How many finance partners were contacted?
- How many draft listings are ready?
- How many rental leads are usable?
- Which lead sources worked best?
- What information was hardest to collect?
- Which sellers/dealers/rental owners need follow-up?
- Is the team ready for week 2 inventory packaging?

## Week 1 Success Definition

Week 1 is successful if the launch operating system is set up and the first useful supply pipeline exists.

Do not judge week 1 only by published listings. Judge it by whether MotoPayee now has a working intake, tracking, and follow-up machine.
