## Trust Education Messages

### Reviewed Listing

A MotoPayee-reviewed listing means our team has checked the basic listing details before publication.

### Seller Verified

Seller Verified means MotoPayee has checked the seller identity or profile information.

### Documents Checked

Documents Checked means MotoPayee has reviewed available ownership or vehicle documents.

### Inspected By MotoPayee

Inspected by MotoPayee means our team has reviewed the vehicle condition and provided a summary.

### Finance Eligible

Finance Eligible means the vehicle has passed MotoPayee's review for possible financing partner routing. Final approval remains subject to partner review.

### Verified Rental

Verified Rental means MotoPayee has reviewed the rental owner, vehicle information, rates, deposit, and rental terms before publishing.

## Things Not To Say

Avoid:

- Guaranteed financing
- Instant loan approval
- All cars are inspected
- All listings are verified
- Cheapest cars in Cameroon
- Any seller can post instantly
- MotoPayee guarantees every transaction
- Send money before review
- Buy cars without risk

Use instead:

- Financing subject to partner review and approval
- Reviewed listings
- Verification available
- Inspected vehicles are clearly marked
- MotoPayee helps reduce risk
- Booking payment and rental terms handled through MotoPayee
