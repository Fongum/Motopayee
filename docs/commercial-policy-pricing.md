# MotoPayee Commercial Policy And Pricing

Reference date: August 2026

## Core Commercial Principle

MotoPayee should grow supply with free listings and free dealer pilots, then monetize trust, payments, financing outcomes, visibility, and transaction support.

MotoPayee should charge where it creates clear value, reduces risk, improves visibility, manages payment, or helps a transaction close.

## Launch Revenue Philosophy

At launch, MotoPayee should prioritize:

- Inventory growth
- Trust building
- Dealer participation
- Rental supply
- Finance partner relationships
- Buyer and renter lead capture

Avoid heavy upfront fees before the marketplace proves demand.

## Free Listing Policy

Basic sale listings are free at launch.

Applies to:

- Individual sellers
- Dealers during pilot

Free listing includes:

- Basic listing intake
- MotoPayee review before publication
- Standard marketplace visibility
- Buyer inquiry routing through MotoPayee where needed

Free listing does not include:

- Guaranteed promotion
- Inspection
- Finance eligibility
- Featured placement
- Assisted closing support

## Dealer Free Pilot Policy

Selected dealers receive a free 6-month pilot.

Dealer receives:

- Dealer profile
- Basic inventory upload support
- MotoPayee-reviewed listings
- Buyer lead routing
- Selected social/WhatsApp promotion
- Finance-eligible review where applicable

Dealer agrees:

- Accurate prices and vehicle details
- Current photos
- Fast updates when vehicles are sold or unavailable
- Prompt response to MotoPayee leads
- Lead handling through MotoPayee during pilot
- No false financeable claims

After pilot, MotoPayee may offer:

- Free limited plan
- Paid dealer subscription
- Featured inventory packages
- Lead analytics
- Finance-focused dealer plan

## Document Verification Policy

Document verification should be free at first.

Reason:

- It supports trust
- It improves listing quality
- It helps finance partner readiness
- It encourages sellers and dealers to cooperate

Future option:

- Keep basic document review free
- Charge for priority review or deeper transaction support

## Inspection Pricing

Inspection can be one of MotoPayee's earliest trust-based revenue products.

Recommended launch pricing:

| Inspection Type | Paid By | Suggested Launch Price |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Buyer-requested inspection | Buyer | 15,000 XAF starting point |
| Seller inspection package | Seller | 15,000-35,000 XAF |
| Finance inspection | Buyer, seller, or bundled | 15,000-35,000 XAF |
| Rental verification visit | Owner or waived during launch | 10,000-25,000 XAF |
| Dealer batch inspection | Dealer or promotional pilot | Negotiated |

Pricing can vary by city, distance, inspection depth, and staff availability.

Inspection rule:

Inspection reduces risk but does not eliminate all risk. MotoPayee should avoid promising perfect mechanical certainty unless a qualified workshop diagnosis is included.

## Featured Listing Pricing

Featured listings should be optional.

Recommended launch pricing:

- 5,000-15,000 XAF per 7-14 days
- Suggested starting point: 10,000 XAF

Featured placement can include:

- Higher listing position
- Social media feature
- WhatsApp status/post inclusion
- Dealer showcase inclusion

Featured placement should not override trust completely. Verified and inspected listings should remain prioritized.

## Assisted Sale Support Fee

Do not push assisted sale fees aggressively at launch.

Possible future model:

- Fixed service fee
- 1-2% success fee
- Buyer/seller split
- Premium transaction support package

Assisted sale support may include:

- Viewing coordination
- Document handover support
- Payment coordination
- Inspection coordination
- Buyer/seller communication support

Recommended launch approach:

- Keep basic inquiry routing free
- Charge only for clearly defined premium support
- Reassess after 90 days

## Rental Commission

Rental bookings are one of the clearest early revenue opportunities because MotoPayee handles booking payment.

Recommended launch model:

- MotoPayee commission: 10% of rental booking value
- Optional renter service fee later
- Featured rental placement later

Commission should be deducted from rental booking value before owner payout.

## Rental Payment And Deposit Handling

Rental payment:

- Renter pays rental amount through MotoPayee
- MotoPayee records booking
- MotoPayee deducts commission
- Owner payout follows confirmation rules

Security deposit:

- Vehicle owner holds the security deposit
- MotoPayee records deposit amount and terms
- MotoPayee records who received the deposit
- Refund rules must be clear before booking

Important:

MotoPayee should not claim to protect or hold rental deposits unless MotoPayee actually holds them.

## Financing Commission Model

Financing should be free for buyers at launch.

MotoPayee should earn from finance partners after successful disbursement.

Recommended launch model:

- No monthly partner fee during pilot
- No charge for incomplete applications
- Success commission after loan disbursement

Future options:

- Qualified lead fee
- Partner portal subscription
- Featured finance partner placement
- Dealer-finance processing partnership

MotoPayee should not rank financing offers only by payment from partners. Buyer fit, response speed, total cost, and partner reliability should matter.

## Insurance Referral Model

Insurance quotes should be free for users at launch.

MotoPayee can earn later through:

- Referral commission
- Quote conversion commission
- Featured insurance partner placement

Insurance should be positioned as a useful add-on, not the main revenue line at launch.

## Import Service Fees

Imports remain secondary for the current strategy cycle.

When activated, possible import revenue:

- MotoPayee service fee
- Quote preparation fee
- Reservation/deposit handling fee
- Milestone payment support
- Broker-assist coordination fee

Imports should stay controlled, partner-backed, and separate from normal local listings.

## Anti-Bypass Commercial Rules

MotoPayee cannot prevent all bypass, but it can reduce bypass by making the platform valuable.

Rules:

- Unverified listing inquiries should route through MotoPayee
- Dealer pilot leads generated by MotoPayee should be handled through MotoPayee
- Financing flows must stay inside MotoPayee to receive support
- Rental booking payment should stay inside MotoPayee
- Inspection and verification records should stay attached to MotoPayee

Buyer-facing principle:

MotoPayee can only support verification, financing, rental payment records, or dispute help when the process remains inside MotoPayee.

Dealer-facing principle:

Leads introduced by MotoPayee should be handled through MotoPayee during the pilot.

## What Not To Monetize Yet

Avoid charging at launch for:

- Basic listing
- Basic dealer participation
- Basic document review
- Buyer browsing
- Buyer financing application
- Initial MFI partner pilot access
- Basic renter inquiry

Reason:

These activities grow marketplace liquidity and partner participation.

## Recommended Launch Defaults

| Item | Launch Policy |
| --- | --- |
| Basic sale listing | Free |
| Dealer pilot | Free for 6 months |
| Document verification | Free at first |
| Buyer-requested inspection | 15,000 XAF starting point |
| Seller inspection package | 15,000-35,000 XAF |
| Featured listing | 10,000 XAF starting point |
| Rental commission | 10% |
| Financing application | Free to buyer |
| Financing revenue | Success commission from partner |
| Insurance quote | Free to user |
| Import fees | Future/secondary |

## 90-Day Review Policy

After 90 days, MotoPayee should review:

- Number of listings published
- Number of verified listings
- Inspection requests
- Featured listing uptake
- Rental bookings
- Rental commission earned
- Financing applications submitted
- Financing partner responses
- Dealer lead quality
- Seller willingness to pay
- Buyer trust feedback
- Bypass patterns

Pricing should be adjusted based on real activity, not assumptions.

## Commercial Summary

Launch free where MotoPayee needs supply and partner participation.

Charge where MotoPayee creates measurable value:

- Inspection
- Featured visibility
- Rental booking payment
- Financing success
- Premium transaction support

Keep the pricing simple, transparent, and tied to trust.
