# MotoPayee Launch Operations Templates

Reference date: August 2026

Use these templates during the first 30-day launch push. Public messaging should position MotoPayee for Cameroon, while internal field execution starts in Buea and Douala.

## Operating Rule

Every lead must be captured, assigned, and moved to one daily status:

- New
- Contacted
- Waiting for photos
- Waiting for price
- Waiting for documents
- Ready for review
- Approved
- Published
- Rejected
- Follow-up later

## 9. Listing Quality Checklist

Before a sale listing is published:

- Seller name and phone captured internally
- Vehicle make, model, year, city, and price present
- Photos are usable
- Seller is reachable
- Basic fraud/spam check passed
- Trust status selected
- Inquiry should route through MotoPayee if unverified

Before a rental listing is published:

- Owner identity checked
- Authority to rent checked
- Vehicle details complete
- Photos received
- Rate and security deposit confirmed
- Self-drive/chauffeur terms clear
- Driver's license requirement stated for self-drive
- Rental vehicle approved by MotoPayee
