# MotoPayee Dealer Pilot Program

Reference date: August 2026

## Positioning

MotoPayee is a trusted vehicle marketplace for Cameroon.

MotoPayee is onboarding selected dealers across Cameroon for a free pilot, with priority operational support starting in key launch cities and expanding city by city.

## Why Dealers Should Join Early

Dealers need more serious buyers, better online presentation, and stronger access to finance-ready customers.

MotoPayee helps dealers:

- Package inventory professionally
- Reach buyers looking for reviewed vehicles
- Promote selected vehicles through MotoPayee channels
- Connect eligible vehicles with financing demand
- Build trust through review, verification, and inspection signals
- Track buyer inquiries more clearly

## Free Pilot Offer

Selected dealers receive a free 6-month pilot.

The pilot includes:

- Dealer profile on MotoPayee
- Basic inventory upload support
- MotoPayee-reviewed vehicle listings
- Buyer inquiry routing
- Social and WhatsApp promotion for selected vehicles
- Finance-eligible vehicle review where applicable
- Lead feedback during the pilot

There is no subscription fee during the pilot.

## What MotoPayee Expects From Dealers

To keep the marketplace trusted, dealers agree to:

- Provide accurate vehicle details
- Provide current photos
- Provide real asking prices
- Update sold or unavailable vehicles quickly
- Respond to MotoPayee-generated leads promptly
- Allow MotoPayee verification where needed
- Avoid false financeable claims
- Handle MotoPayee-generated leads through MotoPayee during the pilot

## Vehicle Listing Standards

Each dealer vehicle should include:

- Make
- Model
- Year
- Price
- City/location
- Mileage where available
- Fuel type
- Transmission
- Clear exterior photos
- Clear interior photos
- Ownership or authority-to-sell status
- Availability status

Incomplete listings may be held for review until the missing information is provided.

## Trust And Verification

MotoPayee may mark listings with trust signals such as:

- MotoPayee Reviewed
- Seller Verified
- Documents Checked
- Inspected by MotoPayee
- Finance Eligible
- Trusted Dealer

Important:

Finance Eligible is assigned only by MotoPayee. A dealer should not claim a vehicle is financeable until MotoPayee has reviewed it against the finance partner criteria.

## Finance-Eligible Opportunity

Finance-eligible vehicles can attract buyers who need financing through MotoPayee partner institutions.

For a dealer, this can create:

- More serious buyer inquiries
- Better conversion from buyers without full cash upfront
- Stronger vehicle credibility
- Cleaner financing application packages

Final financing approval remains subject to partner review and approval.

## Lead Handling Rules

During the pilot, MotoPayee-generated leads should be handled through MotoPayee.

This protects:

- Lead tracking
- Buyer support
- Financing application flow
- Inspection coordination
- Marketplace trust
- Dealer performance reporting

If a buyer first discovers the vehicle through MotoPayee, the dealer should not bypass MotoPayee during the pilot process.

## What Happens After The Pilot

At the end of the free pilot, MotoPayee and the dealer review:

- Number of vehicles listed
- Buyer inquiries received
- Lead response quality
- Finance-eligible vehicle performance
- Sales or serious negotiations generated
- Dealer satisfaction

Possible next steps:

- Continue with free basic listing access with limited features
- Move to a paid dealer plan
- Add featured inventory promotion
- Add finance-focused dealer partnership
- Pause if the dealer is not a fit

## Possible Paid Dealer Plans After Pilot

Future paid plans may include:

- Larger inventory capacity
- Featured dealer profile
- Featured vehicle placement
- Priority verification support
- Lead analytics
- Bulk upload support
- Finance lead prioritization
- Social promotion packages

The pilot is designed to prove value before subscription fees begin.

## Dealer Meeting Checklist

Before or during the meeting, collect:

- Dealer/business name
- Owner or manager name
- Phone and WhatsApp
- City and address
- Inventory contact person
- Number of vehicles available
- Vehicle categories
- Current sales process
- Interest in financing buyers
- Willingness to join free pilot
- Agreement to listing standards
- Agreement to lead handling expectations

## Dealer Vehicle Batch Checklist

For the first batch, request:

- 10-20 vehicles if available
- Prices
- Photos
- Mileage where available
- Vehicle locations
- Availability status
- Documents or ownership status where available
- Vehicles that may be good financing candidates

## Suggested Dealer Meeting Script

Opening:

MotoPayee is building a trusted vehicle marketplace for Cameroon. We are onboarding selected dealers for a free pilot so we can help package inventory professionally and connect dealers with serious buyers.

Value:

During the pilot, MotoPayee helps upload and promote your vehicles, route buyer inquiries, and identify vehicles that may qualify for financing partner demand.

Dealer expectation:

In return, we need accurate prices, current photos, fast updates when vehicles are sold, and proper handling of MotoPayee-generated leads.

Pilot ask:

We would like to start with a free 6-month pilot. You provide an initial batch of vehicles, and we help package and promote them through MotoPayee.
